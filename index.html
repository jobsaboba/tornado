Every ★★★★★ horse in races with 8+ runners — that's it. Blue box: the odds. The result — WIN · PLACE · LOST — lands next to each selection as it comes in.
Tornado's ledger: £10 bank · Jacobs Ladder per day — the day's bet is (first + last) × your unit (ladder 1-2-2 at 10p = 30p a horse). Day returns under the total stake and the ladder climbs (1-2-2-3 → 40p); over it, the ladder shrinks; a new bank high resets to 1-2-2. Each-way at Ladbrokes: ½ win · ½ place at 1/5 odds.
